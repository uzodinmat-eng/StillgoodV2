# Stillgood: Open Questions & Technical Considerations

## 1. NAFDAC Compliance: Best-Before vs. Expiration
- **Question**: How does NAFDAC treat the retail sale of goods past Best-Before vs past Expiry date in Nigeria?
- **Current Policy**: Stillgood strictly prevents listing items past their Best-Before or Expiry date. Products are automatically delisted 24 hours prior to the date on the packaging.
- **Action Item**: Verify standard operational guidelines with Abuja food safety consultants.

## 2. Store Cold Chain Management
- **Question**: For dairy, frozen poultry, and chilled beverages, how do we ensure items stay refrigerated during Abuja power outages?
- **Current Policy**: Only verified supermarkets with 24/7 dedicated generator/solar cold-storage infrastructure (e.g. Grand Square, H-Medix, Next Cash & Carry) are approved for chilled/frozen items.

## 3. Pickup Fulfillment Discrepancies
- **Question**: What happens if a retail customer buys the last item physically from the shelf before a Stillgood pickup customer arrives?
- **Current Policy**: Supermarkets reserve items in a designated "Stillgood Pickup Bin" behind customer care within 15 minutes of order placement. If an item is missing upon arrival, an instant 100% refund is credited back to the customer's payment method or Stillgood Wallet.

## 4. Drift Pricing Rate Tuning
- **Question**: Is 2.5% weekly decay optimal for high-turnover grocery items?
- **Current Policy**: Base drift is 2.5% per week from list date, with an accelerated curve when less than 7 days remain.
